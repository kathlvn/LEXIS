The Legal Office

The Legal Office of Eastern Samar State University provides legal advice, legal research, review of agreements and contracts, investigations, and the drafting of legal documents for the University's offices and officials.

Request a Legal Service File a Complaint or Referral

Who may request

Legal services are for ESSU offices, personnel, and officials, upon a formal written request of the office or department head or key official (Citizen's Charter). Students may request legal research. Complaints and referrals may be filed by anyone, including persons and agencies outside ESSU. Legal Office services are free of charge.

How to request a legal service

  1. 1. Sign in. Open My Requests and enter your email address, then the one-time code we send you. The first time, tell us who you are. No email address? Visit the Legal Office and staff will file your request for you.

  2. 2. Describe your request. Choose the service and describe your concern.

  3. 3. Upload your documents. The page lists the documents each service requires; all required documents must be included. ESSU clients include, once, a signed endorsement letter from their office head (or dean, for students).

  4. 4. Follow your request. In My Requests you can see the status, download documents released to you, and message the Legal Office.

Services offered

Processing periods are counted in working days, from the next working day after your request is submitted. Weekends and holidays are not counted.

About the Legal Office

Mission: to provide responsive, quality legal services that ensure compliance with the law and enable ESSU to carry out its mandate with confidence in its rights, privileges, and protections.

Established in January 2024, the Legal Office is under the Office of the University President and is headed by the Attorney IV.
