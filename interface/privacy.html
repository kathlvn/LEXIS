      Who we are

      The Legal Office of Eastern Samar State University (ESSU Legal Office), College of Law Building, ESSU Main Campus, Borongan City, Eastern Samar, Philippines 6800, decides how and why your personal information is processed in LEXIS. We follow the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its implementing rules.

      What we collect

      • Your details: your name, client category (for example, ESSU personnel, student, or outside client), your ESSU office or outside agency or organization, your position, and your email address. If staff file a request for you and you have no email address, your contact number instead.
      • Your requests: the service you ask for, the description of your concern, the documents you upload (including an endorsement letter from your office head), your messages with the Legal Office, the documents the Legal Office releases to you, and the history of each request. Each request keeps a copy of your details as they were when you submitted it.
      • Verification: whether the Legal Office has verified who you are, how, by which staff member, and any note staff wrote about it.
      • Your consent: the date and time you gave your Data Privacy consent.
      • Signing in: the email address a sign-in code was sent to, when, and how many attempts were made. Codes and sign-in cookies are stored only in scrambled (hashed) form.
      • Security records: if LEXIS detects possible misuse, such as too many attempts in a short time, it records the time and the network (IP) address it came from.

      Your concern and documents may include sensitive personal information, such as information about an offense or a case. Please give only what the Legal Office needs to act on your request.

      Why we collect it, and on what basis

      We use your information only to:

      • confirm who you are and whether you may use a service;
      • receive, act on, and keep track of your requests;
      • contact you about your requests;
      • keep the Legal Office's official record; and
      • keep LEXIS secure.

      We process it on the basis of your consent, given when you complete your profile or sign the Legal Service Request Form at the Legal Office, and because it is needed for ESSU, a public institution, to carry out the Legal Office's functions (Section 12 of the Data Privacy Act). Sensitive personal information is processed with your consent and where it is needed to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims or rights (Section 13).

      We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or make decisions about you by automated means alone.

      Who sees it

      • Legal Office staff who are authorized to handle requests: the attorneys and legal assistants.
      • Others within ESSU, only where needed to act on your request, for example an office asked to respond in an investigation.
      • Email. Sign-in codes and updates on your requests are sent through an email service provider, so they pass through that provider's servers. These emails contain your Request ID, the update, and any note. When the Legal Office writes you a message, the email only says there is one; you read it in My Requests. The emails never contain the description you submitted or your files.
      • Document storage. The documents in LEXIS are planned to be kept in a Google Shared Drive of the Legal Office's ESSU Google Workspace account, so they would be stored on Google's servers. This is subject to the approval of the Legal Office and may still change.
      • When the law requires it, for example by a court order or a request of the National Privacy Commission.

      How we protect it

      • Only authorized staff can open LEXIS's staff pages, and important staff actions on a request are recorded with the staff member's name.
      • You sign in with a one-time code sent to your email; there is no password to steal. Your session ends after 30 minutes without activity, or 2 hours at most.
      • Uploaded files cannot be opened by a public link; they open only for you and authorized staff.
      • LEXIS limits repeated attempts and records possible misuse.

      How long we keep it

      We keep your requests, documents, and details as long as ESSU's records rules and the National Archives of the Philippines' General Records Disposition Schedule require, then dispose of them securely. [Retention period: to be confirmed by the Legal Office]

      Sign-in codes expire 10 minutes after they are sent and can be used only once.

      Your rights

      Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to:

      • be informed about how your information is processed;
      • see the information we hold about you, and get a copy;
      • have wrong or out-of-date information corrected;
      • object to processing, or withdraw your consent;
      • ask that your information be blocked or deleted;
      • be compensated for damages caused by wrong or unlawful processing; and
      • file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

      Some of these rights are limited by law. For example, information that is part of the Legal Office's official record must be kept for the period the records rules set, even if you withdraw your consent. Withdrawing consent does not undo processing done before it.

      Cookies

      LEXIS uses only the cookies needed to keep you signed in. It has no advertising or tracking cookies and no analytics. See Cookies for the full list.

      Contact us

      For questions about this notice, or to use your rights, email the ESSU Legal Office at legaloffice@essu.edu.ph, or visit the Legal Office at the College of Law Building, ESSU Main Campus, Borongan City, Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, except holidays.
