      What a cookie is

      A cookie is a small piece of data that a website asks your browser to keep and send back on your next visit. LEXIS uses cookies only so it knows you have signed in.

      The cookies LEXIS uses

      The cookies LEXIS uses
      CookieWho gets itWhat it is forHow long it lasts
      lois_track Clients Keeps you signed in to My Requests after you enter your one-time code. Ends after 30 minutes without activity, 2 hours at most, or when you press Sign out.
      lois_submit New clients Lets you finish telling us who you are after you confirm your email address with a code. 30 minutes, or until you finish.
      lois_session Legal Office staff Keeps staff signed in to the staff pages. Ends after 30 minutes without activity, 12 hours at most, or when staff log out.

      These cookies hold only a random code, not your name or email address. Your browser sends them only to LEXIS, and the pages' own scripts cannot read them.

      Why there is no cookie banner

      LEXIS cannot keep you signed in without these cookies, and it uses no others, so there is nothing to choose. You can delete them in your browser's settings at any time; you will then need to sign in again.

      Other browser storage (staff only)

      On the staff pages, LEXIS remembers in the browser's local storage (sidebarCollapsed) whether a staff member has collapsed the side menu. It holds only that setting and is never sent to LEXIS. Clients' pages store nothing in the browser besides the cookies above.

      No tracking

      • No analytics, advertising, or social media cookies or scripts.
      • No cookies from other websites.
      • The fonts and the "I am not a robot" check are served by LEXIS itself, so using them sends nothing to another company. The robot check sets no cookie.

      More information

      How we handle your personal information is explained in our Privacy Notice. For questions, email the ESSU Legal Office at legaloffice@essu.edu.ph.
