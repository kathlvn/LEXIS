How drafts, publishing and hiding work

Anyone on staff can write a draft and edit their own drafts. The Head Attorney can edit any draft.

Only the Head Attorney publishes an article, hides it, or changes it once it has been published. A published article is on the public Home page (the three newest) and on the public Articles page.

The public sees “The Legal Office” and the date it was first published, never the name of the staff member who wrote it.

Articles are never deleted: a published article can be hidden and published again later.
