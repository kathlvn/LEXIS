How the figures are counted Periods, Philippine time, working days, holidays, and what each column means.

A request belongs to a period if it was submitted in that period (Philippine time). Completed, pending and overdue show those requests’ state as of today; pending means not yet completed, cancelled or rejected.

Average processing time counts working days from submission to completion, skipping weekends and the dates on the Holidays page. Overdue does not apply to services with no set period.

Staff workload is by the staff member each request is assigned to now. Office and requesting party are the client’s details as filed with each request.

Accomplished requests are the exception: they are the requests completed in the period, whenever they were filed (completion = the last change to Completed). Contract reviews list the “Review of MOA/MOU, Contracts” requests filed in the period, plus any filed earlier that are still open.

The Client Satisfaction Measurement counts surveys clients answered in the period (offered after a request is completed; optional). Satisfied means Agree or Strongly agree, out of the answers that are not “Not applicable”; the overall rate counts every rated statement together. Results show only totals, and only from 3 responses, so no single client’s answers can be seen.
