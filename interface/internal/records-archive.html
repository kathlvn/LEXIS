After downloading

  1. Unzip it and upload the whole LEXIS-Archive-… folder to the Legal Office's Google Drive archive folder.
  2. Check that the folder is shared with Legal Office staff only.
  3. Delete the ZIP and the unzipped copy from this computer, including the Downloads folder and the Recycle Bin or Trash.

The archive has personal information and legal records (Data Privacy Act of 2012). Every export is recorded with your name.
