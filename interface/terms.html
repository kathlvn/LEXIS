      In short

      • LEXIS is free. It never asks for payment.
      • Give true information and only documents you may share.
      • This site is general information, not legal advice.
      • For urgent legal deadlines, contact the Legal Office directly.

      About these terms

      By using LEXIS, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use it; you may visit the Legal Office instead. How we handle your personal information is explained in our Privacy Notice.

      What LEXIS is for

      LEXIS lets you send legal service requests and complaints or referrals to the ESSU Legal Office, follow their status, exchange messages and documents with the Legal Office, and receive the documents it releases to you. Legal Office services are free of charge. LEXIS never asks for or accepts payment, so there is nothing to refund.

      Who may use it

      • Legal services are for ESSU offices, personnel, and officials, upon a formal written request of the office or department head or key official (Citizen's Charter). ESSU clients include a signed endorsement letter from their office head until the Legal Office has verified them.
      • Students may request legal research only.
      • Complaints and referrals may be filed by anyone, including persons and agencies outside ESSU.

      Each service lists who may use it and the documents it requires. LEXIS may limit how many requests can be filed in a day, or kept open while you are not yet verified. If you have no email address, visit the Legal Office and staff will file your request for you.

      Signing in

      You sign in with your email address and a one-time code we send to it. Use your own email address, keep your codes to yourself, and press Sign out when you are done, especially on a shared or public computer. Tell the Legal Office right away if you think someone else has used your email to reach LEXIS.

      Accurate information

      The details, descriptions, and documents you give must be true, complete, and your own or ones you are allowed to share. An endorsement letter must be genuinely signed by the office head it names. Giving false information or documents may lead the Legal Office to decline your request, and may make you liable under the law and, for ESSU personnel and students, under University rules.

      This site is not legal advice

      The pages of this site and the automatic messages LEXIS sends are general information only, not legal advice. Legal advice comes only from a Legal Office attorney acting on a request.

      The Legal Office serves Eastern Samar State University in its official capacity. Filing a request does not make the Legal Office your private lawyer, and the Legal Office does not represent the person who files a complaint or referral. If you need a lawyer for a personal matter, consult a private lawyer or the Public Attorney's Office.

      Processing periods and urgent matters

      Processing periods are counted in working days, from the next working day after your request is submitted. Some services, such as legal research, have no set period. The Legal Office may ask you for more information or documents; please reply quickly so your request is not delayed.

      Do not rely on LEXIS alone for a matter with an urgent legal deadline. Contact or visit the Legal Office directly.

      The Legal Office may decline a request

      The Legal Office may decline, return, or cancel a request, for example when it is outside the Legal Office's functions, the required documents or endorsement are missing, the client cannot be verified, or LEXIS is being misused. When it does, the Legal Office tells you the reason.

      Misuse

      Do not:

      • pretend to be someone else, or file a request for someone without their authority;
      • submit false, forged, or altered documents;
      • upload files with viruses or other harmful content;
      • try to see requests, files, or accounts that are not yours;
      • use automated tools to send requests or codes, or try to overload or break into LEXIS; or
      • send abusive or harassing messages.

      If LEXIS is misused, the Legal Office may decline or close the requests involved, withdraw a client's verification, limit access, keep a record of what happened, and refer the matter to the proper authorities or, for ESSU personnel and students, to the University.

      Your files

      Upload only files you are allowed to share. Files and messages you send become part of the Legal Office's record of your request and are kept as explained in the Privacy Notice.

      Availability

      We try to keep LEXIS available, but it may be unavailable at times, for example during maintenance. If you cannot reach it, email or visit the Legal Office.

      Changes and contact

      We may update these terms. The date at the top shows the latest version. For questions, email the ESSU Legal Office at legaloffice@essu.edu.ph, or visit the Legal Office at the College of Law Building, ESSU Main Campus, Borongan City, Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, except holidays.
