Historical background

Under the Department of Budget and Management's National Budget Circular No. 589 (May 25, 2022), on the revised Organization and Staffing Standards for state universities and colleges, ESSU created and filled the positions of Attorney IV and Legal Assistants I to III. The appointments were signed on November 3, 2023, and the Board of Regents confirmed the appointment of the Attorney IV through Resolution No. 224, s. 2024.

The Legal Office began operations in January 2024 at its office in the Administration Building, offering legal services to the University. In 2025, it served a growing number of requests, working with the Office of the Solicitor General (OSG) and the legal divisions of other government agencies. The office is now located in the College of Law Building.

Office goals

The Legal Office aims to serve the University's legal needs with excellence, integrity, and accountability, and to support legal compliance and due process. To this end, it seeks to:

  • Deliver legal service within the rule of law
  • Provide a forum for the resolution of issues
  • Pursue all lawful and alternative actions, with court action as a last resort
  • Build linkages with government agencies and the community for law and order

Service outcomes

  • Provide the University the legal services it needs
  • Offer accessible legal support for all its legal undertakings
  • Serve as a fair ground where matters can be resolved amicably
  • Build the competence of office staff as a foundation for growth
  • Instill in staff and clients the ethics and responsibilities of the legal profession
  • Develop an ESSU community that adheres to the law

Organizational structure

The Legal Office is under the Office of the University President. It is headed by the Attorney IV, assisted by Legal Assistants appointed by the University President.

Attorney IV

Heads the office. Acts as the University's legal adviser; represents the University before courts and administrative bodies, including when deputized by the OSG; sets standards for legal opinions and memoranda; reviews legal documents; and develops the office's records management policies.

Legal Assistant III

Maintains case files, researches laws, rules, and jurisprudence, schedules the office's activities, receives and routes documents, and records meeting proceedings.

Legal Assistants I and II

Prepare orders and notices for cases, research laws and jurisprudence, maintain the case-status database, prepare correspondence, clearances, and certifications, and log and follow up on concerns brought to the office.
