Data Privacy Notice · full text

Who collects it. The Legal Office of Eastern Samar State University (ESSU Legal Office) collects your name, category, office or agency, position, email address, and later the details and files of your requests.

Why. Only to verify who you are, receive and act on your requests, contact you about them, and keep the Legal Office's official record, as allowed under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

Where it is kept. In LEXIS, the Legal Office's system. Only authorized Legal Office staff can see your details and requests.

Sharing. Your information is not shared outside the University, except where needed to act on your request or where the law requires it. Sign-in codes and updates are emailed to you through an email service provider; the emails never contain the description of your concern or your files.

How long. For as long as ESSU's records rules and the National Archives' disposition schedules require.

Cookies. Only the cookies needed to keep you signed in. No advertising or tracking cookies (Cookies).

Your rights. You may ask the ESSU Legal Office to let you see, correct, or (subject to record-keeping rules) delete your information, and you may file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission. Contact: legaloffice@essu.edu.ph.
